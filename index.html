About

A Journey to Awareness is a three-screen immersive installation that visualizes a Buddhist meditation journey through abstract digital visuals and a carefully composed multisensory environment. Combining digital media techniques with physical elements such as lighting, scent, and spatial arrangements, the installation engages the visual, auditory, olfactory, and tactile senses to create an immersive meditative experience.

The installation offers audiences a glimpse into the stages and experiences of meditation, from sensory overload to awareness, while exploring meditation as a potential method for self-reflection and easing internal struggles. Through the interplay of audiovisual elements and sensory stimuli, the experience invites audiences to turn their attention inward and reflect on their inner experiences.

This project was created by Do Hoang Lam Nguyen and Truong Thuy Mai Hong as part of the Heightened Multisensory Experiences course of the School of Design, RMIT University, Melbourne, under the mentorship of Dr. Darrin Verhagen and Dr. Niklas Pajanti.
